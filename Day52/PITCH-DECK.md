# AI Career Copilot — Pitch Deck (Recreated Outline)

> Recreated on Day 52 as a lightweight reference — not a redesign of the slides, just enough substance to keep today's technical decisions aligned with the pitch narrative.

1. **Cover** — AI Career Copilot: Your AI co-pilot for landing your first job in India.
2. **Problem** — Indian students get filtered out by ATS systems, don't know how to fix their LinkedIn, and have no structured way to prepare for interviews or know what skills they're missing — and existing tools are priced in USD.
3. **Solution** — One INR-priced platform: ATS resume scoring, JD match, LinkedIn optimization, interview prep, and skill gap analysis.
4. **Market** — Millions of students graduating annually in India; placement season creates predictable demand spikes.
5. **Product Demo (narrative)** — Upload resume → get ATS score → paste JD → see gaps → fix LinkedIn → practice interview → see skill roadmap.
6. **Differentiation** — INR pricing + India-specific framing vs. Jobscan/Teal/LinkedIn Premium (USD-priced, not campus-pipeline aware).
7. **Business Model** — Freemium: limited free scans/month, paid tier unlocks unlimited scans + full interview prep + skill roadmap, priced in INR.
8. **Traction (current)** — Concept validated informally with peers; 60-day build-in-public challenge as credibility signal; no paying customers yet (validation stage).
9. **Roadmap** — v1.0 (this build) → placement-cell B2B dashboards → mobile app → regional language support.
10. **Ask / Ambition** — Founder-led build; goal is to reach first paying users during the next campus placement cycle.
