Health Impact Analysis — Delhi

How Delhi's air (AQI 91 today, annual avg 179) and water (TDS 400–600 ppm) affect your body.

🫁 Air Quality — Body Impact

💧 Water Quality — Hair & Skin

🌬 Air Pollution Deep Dive

At Delhi's annual average AQI of 179 (Moderate-Poor), PM2.5 particles (≤2.5 µm) penetrate deep into lung tissue, entering the bloodstream. Long-term exposure at this level is associated with a 26% higher risk of respiratory disease. Even on "good" days like today (AQI 91), PM2.5 at 41 µg/m³ is 2.7× WHO's safe limit of 15 µg/m³.

Winter months (Oct–Jan) push Delhi AQI into 300–500 (Very Poor–Severe) range due to crop burning, vehicle emissions, and temperature inversions trapping pollutants near the ground.

🚿 Hard Water Deep Dive

Delhi's water TDS of 400–600 ppm far exceeds the 200 ppm threshold considered safe for hair washing. Calcium and magnesium ions bind to hair shafts, ruffling cuticles and reducing tensile strength — leading to breakage and increased shedding. Chlorine added for disinfection further strips natural scalp oils.

Studies show hair washed with hard water has 4× higher magnesium deposits and measurably lower tensile strength vs hair washed with softened water. Some outer Delhi areas (Rohini, Chhatarpur) see TDS exceed 1000 ppm from borewell sources.

⚡ Biggest Anomaly

📉

Delhi Today vs Annual Average Gap

Today's AQI of 91 is 52 points lower than the 7-day average (143) and 88 points below the annual average (179). This is a misleading improvement — seasonal pattern. Delhi regularly bounces between "Satisfactory" in June and "Severe" in December. Don't be fooled by today's reading.

😮 Most Surprising Observation

💧

Water Risk Outweighs Air Risk in Summer

In June, when AQI is relatively better, Delhi's water quality becomes the #1 health threat. Monsoon runoff contaminates pipes, borewell levels drop causing TDS spikes, and chlorination increases — tripling waterborne disease risk vs winter. Most residents focus on air purifiers but neglect shower filters, which matter more in summer.

📌 Key Trend

📈

Delhi AQI Worsening Year-on-Year

Delhi's annual AQI has climbed from 154 (2020) → 162 (2021) → 174 (2022) → 164 (2023) → 178 (2024) → 179 (2026). A +6.5% average worsening despite government interventions. Vehicle numbers and construction activity continue to outpace pollution control efforts.

Personalized Recommendations

Actionable steps to protect your health in Delhi's air and water environment.

☀️ Daily Air Actions

  • Check AQI before going outdoors — use AQI.in or SAFAR app. Target outdoor time when AQI <75.
  • Wear N95/KN95 mask for outdoor activities when AQI exceeds 100 — surgical masks block only 30% of PM2.5.
  • Avoid outdoor exercise 7–10 AM and 6–9 PM — peak traffic hours spike localised PM2.5 by 40–60%.
  • Keep car cabin air filter clean — replace every 6 months in Delhi (vs 12 months manufacturer standard).
  • Shower immediately after returning from outdoors to remove particulate deposits from skin and hair.

🏠 Indoor Air Improvements

  • Run a HEPA air purifier in your bedroom and main living area — target rooms you spend 8+ hours in.
  • Place 2–3 indoor plants (Peace Lily, Snake Plant, Spider Plant) — proven to reduce VOCs by up to 25%.
  • Keep windows closed from 6–10 AM and during high-traffic or windy dusty periods.
  • Use exhaust fans while cooking — gas stoves produce NO₂ and PM2.5 indoors.
  • Vacuum with HEPA filter 2× per week; mop floors to capture settled dust vs resuspending it.

💇 Hair Care for Hard Water

  • Install a shower filter with activated carbon + KDF media — removes chlorine, reduces calcium deposits.
  • Use a sulfate-free shampoo to avoid stripping already-stressed hair of its natural oils.
  • Do an apple cider vinegar rinse (1:2 ratio) weekly — dissolves mineral buildup and restores scalp pH.
  • Apply a chelating or clarifying shampoo once per week to remove accumulated mineral deposits.
  • Oil massage (coconut or castor) 1–2× weekly creates a barrier against hard-water mineral bonding.
  • Consider final rinse with filtered/RO water — even once weekly noticeably reduces breakage.

🧴 Skin Care Recommendations

  • Moisturise immediately after bathing (within 3 minutes) to lock in hydration before hard water dries skin.
  • Use a gentle, fragrance-free cleanser — harsh soaps worsen the stripping effect of high-TDS water.
  • Niacinamide serum helps repair barrier function disrupted by pollution + hard water combination.
  • Apply SPF 50 daily — pollution increases photo-sensitivity and UV damage significantly.
  • Antioxidant-rich diet (Vitamin C, E) combats free radicals introduced by PM2.5 exposure.

💧 Water Quality Improvements

  • Install RO + UV + MTDS purifier for drinking water if TDS >500 ppm in your area.
  • Use a shower/tap filter for bathing — specifically designed for Indian hard water (SF-15 or equivalent).
  • Check your area's TDS with a basic meter (₹200–500 online) — readings vary widely by colony.
  • During monsoon (July–Sept), increase purification: add a pre-filter sediment cartridge for extra turbidity.
  • Test borewell water annually if your building uses it — TDS can spike above 1000 ppm in outer Delhi.

🏃 Outdoor Activity Guidance

  • Best outdoor window: 10 AM–12 PM in summer when AQI tends to be lowest (convection disperses pollutants).
  • Run or cycle in parks, not arterial roads — roadside PM2.5 can be 3–5× higher than park levels.
  • On Severe AQI days (>300, common Nov–Jan): skip outdoor exercise entirely, use indoor alternatives.
  • Post-exercise: rinse nasal passages with saline spray to remove trapped particulates.
  • Hydrate extra after outdoor exercise — pollution and heat accelerate dehydration in Delhi summers.
