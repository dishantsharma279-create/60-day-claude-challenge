# Day 47 – Content Intelligence Studio

## 📌 Overview
Today I built **Content Intelligence Studio**, a premium AI-powered content analysis platform that reviews text and images using multiple specialized AI reviewers. The application provides actionable feedback, content optimization, AI-generated rewrites, platform-specific recommendations, and an executive summary through a modern SaaS-style dashboard.

---

## 🚀 Features
- AI-powered content analysis
- Multi-stage review workflow
- Text & image upload support
- Platform-specific recommendations
- Overall content score
- Strengths & weaknesses analysis
- AI-generated content rewrite
- Alternative hooks & titles
- Publishing checklist
- Executive summary
- Before vs After comparison
- Live activity log
- Responsive dark UI

---

## 🧠 Specialized AI Reviewers
- Hook Strategist
- Structure & Readability Expert
- Brand Voice Auditor
- Visual Content Analyst
- Engagement Psychologist
- Algorithm & Platform Critic
- Chief Strategist (Final Report)
